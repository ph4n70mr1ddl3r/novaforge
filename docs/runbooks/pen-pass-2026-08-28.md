## Findings (triaged, the §8 discipline — both closed same-day, 2026-08-28)

- **PF-1 (low) — CLOSED: hook existence enumerable on the anonymous webhook route.**
  A nonexistent hook answered 404/4004 while a known hook with a bad signature
  answered 401/4012 — an anonymous caller could distinguish "hook exists" from
  "signature wrong". `InboundProcessor.receive` now routes every unknown-address
  form (unknown hook id, unbound entity, malformed tenant id) through the same
  HMAC checks against an empty active set, rendering the wrong-secret leg's exact
  `SIGNATURE_INVALID` — the anonymous surface never confirms or denies an
  address. Pinned by `IntegrationWebhookTests.unknownAddressIndistinguishable`
  (the unknown-hook and malformed-tenant answers are byte-identical to the
  known-hook wrong-secret control).
- **PF-2 (low) — CLOSED: wrong-shape webhook URLs answer 500 INTERNAL.**
  `POST /api/v1/webhooks/inbound/{single-segment}` (not the
  `/{tenant}/{entity}/{hookId}` shape) surfaced the container's NoResourceFound as
  500 instead of 404 — noise for monitoring, no information leak. The integration
  service's ProblemAdvice maps `NoResourceFoundException` to 404 problem+json
  (code 4004). Pinned by
  `IntegrationWebhookTests.wrongShapeRouteAnswersNotFound`.
